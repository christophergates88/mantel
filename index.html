Nobody else's business

We can't leak what we never have.

Most life apps keep your records on their servers, link your bank through a middleman, and run your data past someone's AI. Breaches of companies holding ID photos and financial data keep coming. Mantel is built the other way around.

On your iPhone only

No account, no sign-up, no cloud of ours. iOS encrypts your records and seals them whenever the phone is locked.

No ads, no trackers

There's no analytics or advertising code in the app. The App Store label reads "Data Not Collected."

No bank login

The money-leak finder reads a statement file you pick, on your phone, then forgets it.

AI that stays home

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, the brief and Ask run on the device. Nothing goes to an AI company.

Check it yourself

Turn on App Privacy Report in iPhone Settings. Mantel shouldn't show up contacting any servers.

Your data outlives us

If Mantel ever went away, nothing would be deleted. Locked backups and spreadsheet exports are yours to keep.

Questions

Where is my information stored?

Only on your iPhone, inside Mantel's private storage, encrypted by iOS. Mantel has no servers and no accounts, so there's nothing of yours anywhere else we could reach.

What happens if I lose my phone?

If your iPhone's iCloud Backup is on, Mantel comes back with your other apps when you restore. You can also make a password-locked backup file in Settings and keep it wherever you like.

How does it find subscriptions without my bank?

Download a statement from your bank or card (CSV works best; PDF works too), then open it in Mantel. It reads the file on your iPhone, finds what repeats, and forgets the file.

Which iPhones does it work on?

iPhones running iOS 18 or later. The optional Apple Intelligence extras need an iPhone 15 Pro or newer; everything else works without them.

Can my spouse use it?

Yes. Premium includes Family Sharing, so one purchase unlocks Premium on up to five family members' iPhones. Each phone keeps its own records; share the Emergency Binder or a backup file to pass information along.

Is this financial, legal or insurance advice?

No. Mantel organizes what you enter and reminds you about it. For advice, talk to a licensed professional.
