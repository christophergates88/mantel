      Here's everything we know about you.

      Nothing.Homesake has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. There's nowhere for your life to go.

      • It all lives on your iPhone.

        Encrypted by iOS and sealed whenever the phone is locked. Lock the app itself with Face ID if you like.

      • No bank login, ever.

        The leak finder reads a statement file you choose, then forgets it. No middleman holds a key to your money.

      • AI that stays home.

        On iPhone 15 Pro and newer, Apple Intelligence writes your brief on the phone. Your records never go to an AI company.

      • Check it yourself.

        iPhone's App Privacy Report lists every server each app contacts. Homesake's list stays empty.
